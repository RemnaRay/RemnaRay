# ADR-021: Purchases only from the balance

- Status: accepted (owner decision О-12, 2026-09-27; design approved 2026-09-29)
- Date: 2026-09-29
- Scope: F37 — payments, invoices, receipts, referral rewards, bot, site,
  console

## Context

The specification lets a customer pay for a plan at a provider or from the
internal balance (FR-020, FR-070). A provider payment activates the plan in
the payment pipeline (11.2), the receipt names the plan (FR-062, 11.5), and a
referral reward follows a purchase, or a top-up when `count_topups` is on
(15.1–15.2).

The shop sells as a self-employed person (НПД) or without a status. For such a
shop the simplest honest model is the one hosting providers use: money comes
in only as balance top-ups, each with one receipt, and the service is bought
from the balance. A provider payment then never activates anything by itself,
and a referral reward follows money actually received — a purchase paid from a
balance that was topped up no longer counts twice (R135).

## Decision

- A plan is bought, renewed and changed only from the balance. A purchase from
  the balance is still an instant invoice with `provider = balance`.
- A provider invoice is always `kind = topup`. When the balance is short, the
  top-up is for the missing amount,
  `max(missing, settings.balance.topup_min_minor, provider minimum)`; a
  missing amount above `topup_max_minor` is invoiced as is. For a plan change
  the missing amount uses the old plan's remainder as of `now + 24 h`.
- A top-up for a plan remembers its purpose (`target_plan_id`, `target_kind`,
  `target_promocode`). After payment the customer buys as a second step: the
  notice's «Купить <тариф>» button opens a confirmation card with a fresh
  quote; nothing is debited by one press.
- One receipt, at the top-up. The receipt line and the provider's payment
  description are one template, default `Пополнение баланса (#{number})`.
- Provider invoices carry a number `NN-00001`: a fixed two-digit provider code
  and a per-provider counter at least five digits wide. Numbers are unique and
  increasing per provider and may have gaps (a failed provider call).
- A discount promocode is applied at the purchase from the balance, not
  reserved at the top-up.
- Referral rewards come only from top-ups; «first purchase» becomes «first
  top-up»; `count_topups` is removed.
- The dashboard shows «Поступления» (top-ups) and «Продажи» (purchases from
  the balance minus refunds).
- Out of scope: the ИП/ООО «advance and offset» scheme; zero-amount purchases
  and the EX-06 credit posting (package 5, R20/R73, О-5); the e-mail request
  before the first receipt (R41, package 8).

## Consequences

- The automatic reward reversal on a refund practically never fires: top-ups
  are not refunded and purchases are no longer reward sources. Protection is
  `hold_hours` and the manual reversal.
- `plans.price_overrides` stays in the database unused.
- An older settings export that still has `referral.count_topups` imports; the
  key is skipped.
- Provider minimum amounts and the way a self-employed receipt travels at
  YooKassa, Robokassa and Lava are verified against current provider
  documentation before the code relies on them.
